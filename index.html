The Project

The generation and evaluation of natural language commentary has been the focus of a large number of studies in the era of LLMs. While we have a near-limitless amount of commentary on any and all subjects, generating text that is informative, clear, and accurate, and which also holds the attention of the reader is a task that has proven to be quite challenging. While LLMs are great at producing human-like text, they often struggle with accuracy, reasoning, long-term analysis, and so on.

Chess is a game that challenges all these aspects, and generating precise, engaging commentary is difficult for even state-of-the-art models. This project evaluates whether providing an LLM with move-by-move information on the features of the current game state can help bridge this gap in LLM commentary, and help players learn and improve on their own games with feature-rich, accurate move-level feedback.

The Study

Each of the moves presented in this study is from famous chess games commentated on by both LLMs and chess experts. In each of the following questions, you are given a board state with the move played, and 4 different comments related to the move. Please rank the commentary in your order of preference, with rank 1 being your most preferred comment.

Each move will also be accompanied with 3 additional questions, whereby you will be asked to rank the 4 commentaries on informativeness and clarity, and assess whether you find the information presented in each commentary to be accurate. If you have any additional feedback on the comments, there will be a space provided after every question where you may provide any such feedback.
